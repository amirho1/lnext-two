## Countdown to the future
The hero connects the mission, next event, and time in a single typographic orbital composition. A light warm-white surface, ink typography, electric violet, and a restrained mint accent balance teenage curiosity with institutional credibility. All visible artwork is CSS geometry, typography, or simple vector shapes. No images, photography, generated imagery, or image illustrations are permitted.

Persian RTL is primary. Vazirmatn is served through next/font (SIL Open Font License). The English wordmark is a typographic concept pending final brand approval.

## Truthful event state
The first event is a grade-nine educational bootcamp in Tehran, proposed for Dey 1405. Its exact day, time, venue, capacity, fee, and programme are unconfirmed. The hero therefore says «به‌زودی» and «در حال برنامه‌ریزی», without fabricated time or scarcity. No sponsors, team members, or relationship with Alavi are claimed as confirmed.

FutureOrbit accepts an optional confirmed startsAt ISO timestamp including an explicit timezone offset or Z. For a Tehran local datetime, supply the confirmed instant with its Asia/Tehran offset (currently +03:30); never pass an ambiguous local string. The display formats the confirmed date in Asia/Tehran. Remaining time is calculated from the absolute instant, updated each second, and clamped to zero. Expiration links to the event's actual status rather than assuming it has ended. Missing or invalid dates remain in planning mode.

Event FAQ uses accessible native disclosure controls. No accounts, registration, checkout, AI features, or invented contact destinations are introduced.

The final contact-form service, approved contact information, organization relationship, and wider pages are not implemented. No false submission-success state is shown. The future production contact form must carry event/cooperation context and must not imply a reserved seat.

## Responsive and motion
Desktop pairs right-aligned narrative with the orbital time composition on the left. Mobile stacks the narrative before the orbit and rearranges event metadata. A slow CSS orbit is decorative, with no content dependent on animation. prefers-reduced-motion disables motion and smooth scrolling. All controls have visible keyboard focus states.
